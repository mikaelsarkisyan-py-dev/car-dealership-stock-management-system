Login/Sign up page:

. automatically load up the login page (that way employees can quickly log in rather than changing from the sign up page to the log in page)
. button at the bottom of the login form for new employees that need to create a new account



login requirments:
. id/username
. password

sign up requirements:
. first name
. second name
. password
. password confirmation
. once signed in, unique id will be automatically generated

ID/USERNAME:
consists of first letter of first name + second name + unique randomly generated 4 digit number

PASSWORD:
must be at least 8 characters long, have capital and lower case letters and at least 1 special character


WHERE TO FIND UNIQUE ID/USERNAME:
top left of the screen will be an icon with user details and stuff, there the user can find what their unique id/username is
